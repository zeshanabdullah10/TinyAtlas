Yosemite Valley

California, United States

Yosemite Valley is a glacial valley in Yosemite National Park in the western Sierra Nevada mountains of Central California, United States. The valley is about 7.5 mi long and 3,000–3,500 ft deep, surrounded by high granite summits such as Half Dome and El Capitan, and densely forested with pines. The valley is drained by the Merced River, and a multitude of streams and waterfalls flow into it, including Tenaya, Illil Wikipedia

Know before you go

Getting there

  • There are no gas or petrol stations in Yosemite Valley, but electric vehicle charging stations are available. Yosemite National Park
  • State Route 120 from the east (Tioga Pass Road) is only open when free of snow, usually May to October, so check road conditions in advance. Yosemite National Park
  • Be sure to fill your gas tank in Oakhurst, or you may pay dearly as you get closer to the park. Yosemite National Park

Getting around

  • During summer months traffic becomes heavily congested in Yosemite Valley, and parking can be nearly impossible to find. Yosemite National Park
  • Fresno Yosemite International Airport has YARTS bus service to Yosemite during the summer months. Yosemite National Park
  • From Merced Airport, take the YARTS shuttle to reach Yosemite. Yosemite National Park

When to go

  • Weather can change rapidly during all seasons and varies greatly with elevation, so pack layered clothing and rain gear. Yosemite National Park
  • Summer is typically warm and dry with occasional thunderstorms, with temperatures from 50°F to the low 90°F range. Yosemite National Park
  • Fall and spring are highly variable; rain is less likely early in fall or late in spring, while rain or snow is likely late in fall or early in spring. Yosemite National Park

Permits and fees

  • Entrance fees are valid for seven days and allow unlimited re-entry for the week. Yosemite National Park
  • Advance reservations are not needed in 2026. Yosemite National Park

Money and connectivity

  • Entrance fees as of 2024 include $35 for private vehicles, with no per person fee. Yosemite National Park

Staying safe

  • During the snow season, usually November through March, snow chains may be required. Yosemite National Park
  • Chain requirements are strictly enforced in Yosemite, with potential fines as much as $5,000, so chains should be carried during those months. Yosemite National Park
