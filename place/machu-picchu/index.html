Machu Picchu

Cusco, Peru

Machu Picchu is a 15th-century Inca citadel located in the Eastern Cordillera of southern Peru on a mountain ridge at 2,430 meters. It is situated in the Machupicchu District of Urubamba Province about 80 kilometers northwest of Cusco, above the Sacred Valley and along the Urubamba River, which forms a deep canyon with a subtropical mountain climate. Know before you go

Getting there

  • The only ways to get to Aguas Calientes are by train or on foot; no roads go there. Machu Picchu
  • From Aguas Calientes, you can reach the ruins by bus or by walking, which is a free steep hike. Machu Picchu
  • Frequent buses leave to the ruins starting at 05:30. Machu Picchu

Getting around

  • There are no vehicles of any kind in the park, so bring comfortable walking shoes. Machu Picchu
  • Many of the paths are one-way, so you can easily miss a spot or be forced into the exit accidentally. Machu Picchu
  • No walking sticks are allowed in the main area, except for visitors who have difficulty walking. Machu Picchu

When to go

  • Most yearly rain falls during the rainy season, which in Machu Picchu is October to April. Machu Picchu
  • The busiest periods are in the dry season from June to August. Machu Picchu
  • The wet season in Peru runs from November until the end of March, so it is best to include extra days for delays. Machu Picchu

Permits and fees

  • The government caps the number of tickets to 5000 per day. Machu Picchu
  • Tickets bought in person may only be used the day after they are purchased. Machu Picchu
  • It is obligatory to bring your passport to enter Machu Picchu. Machu Picchu

Altitude and health

  • There are very high levels of UV-radiation, so take precautions. Machu Picchu
  • Unless you have acclimated to the high altitude, you will likely be out of breath on any ascent. Machu Picchu

Money and connectivity

  • Bus tickets cost US$12 each way or US$24 round-trip for adult foreigners. Machu Picchu
  • A Machu Picchu ticket costs S/152. Machu Picchu
  • Toilets outside the entrance cost S/2. Machu Picchu
