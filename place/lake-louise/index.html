Know before you go

Getting there

  • Lake Louise is 186 km west of Calgary and 56 km northwest of Banff on Highway 1. Lake Louise
  • An alternative from Banff is the Bow Valley Parkway (Highway 1A), a narrower tree-lined road with a better chance of seeing wildlife such as bears and deer. Lake Louise
  • Calgary International Airport is a 2½-hour drive to Lake Louise, and shuttles can drop off at hotels in Banff and Lake Louise. Lake Louise

Getting around

  • The Icefields Parkway connects Lake Louise to Jasper and is 230 km (142 mi). Lake Louise
  • To reach the Lake Louise lakeshore, book the Parks Canada Shuttle in advance, park at the Park and Ride at Lake Louise Ski Resort, and take a bus every 20 minutes. Lake Louise
  • Private vehicles are forbidden from driving up to Moraine Lake, so you must reserve a shuttle in advance. Lake Louise

When to go

  • Before 8AM you can see Lake Louise with few people around; just before dusk is also reasonably quiet. Lake Louise
  • Lake Louise is best seen first thing in the morning before other tourists arrive, or in the early evening after they have gone. Lake Louise
  • At Moraine Lake, coach groups start to arrive by 8:30AM. Lake Louise

Money and connectivity

  • Parking at Lake Louise costs $36.75 (2025) and fills up before sunrise, remaining full until sunset. Lake Louise
