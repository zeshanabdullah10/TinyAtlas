Zermatt

Valais, Switzerland

Zermatt is a municipality in the district of Visp in the German-speaking section of the canton of Valais in Switzerland. It has a year-round population of about 5,800 and is classified as a town by the Swiss Federal Statistical Office. Know before you go

Getting there

  • Private cars can only drive as far as Täsch, and the last 7 km must be travelled by train or taxi. Zermatt
  • A shuttle train departs from the Matterhorn Terminal Täsch every 20 minutes during the day, and there are 2,100 covered parking spaces. Zermatt
  • Half-hourly trains run from Visp, which has connections to major Swiss cities and the Geneva and Zurich airports. Zermatt

Getting around

  • Zermatt has almost no internal combustion vehicles except occasional delivery and specialist services. Zermatt
  • Buses run every 20-30 minutes depending on the time of day and year. Zermatt
  • In general, anything in the village is at most a twenty minute walk away if you are fit. Zermatt

When to go

  • During the high season, nearly 20,000 people are living in a town with only 5 or 6 streets, so expect crowds. Zermatt

Money and connectivity

  • Local bus trips cost Fr. 2.50 on the green line and Fr. 3.20 on the red line, but are free with a ski pass or train ticket. Zermatt
  • A weekly pass with unlimited rides costs Fr. 22. Zermatt
  • Zermatt is busier and much more expensive than other destinations in the region. Zermatt

Staying safe

  • You can cycle or stroll in complete safety. Zermatt
  • Almost all vehicles in Zermatt are battery driven and almost completely silent. Zermatt
