Fairy Meadows

Nanga Parbat, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan

The Fairy Meadows, locally known as Joot, is an area of grassland near one of the base camp sites of Nanga Parbat, located in Diamer District in the Gilgit-Baltistan region in Pakistan. At an altitude of about 3,300 metres above sea level, it serves as the launching point for mountaineers summiting Nanga Parbat by the Rakhiot face. In 1995, the Government of Pakistan declared Fairy Meadows a National Park. Since 2021 Wikipedia

Know before you go

Getting there

  • The Pakistani section of the highway officially starts at Hasan Abdal, 50 km northwest of Rawalpindi, while the Chinese section starts from Kashgar. Karakoram Highway
  • The usual jumping-off point for the highway is Gilgit, and you can take a PIA flight from Islamabad to Gilgit. Karakoram Highway
  • From Rawalpindi, a bus journey to Hasan Abdal may take not more than 1 hour. Karakoram Highway

Getting around

  • The most used method of travel is hopping on to vans, which is affordable and convenient. Karakoram Highway
  • The border is not open for cyclists; from the Pakistani side you can cycle up to the pass but not over it and must return to Sost to take the bus. Karakoram Highway
  • In some narrow places, only one vehicle can pass at a time while the other waits on the narrow edge of the road. Karakoram Highway

When to go

  • The Khunjerab Pass is closed from January through to April due to heavy snow. Karakoram Highway
  • Landslides that can block the road for hours or more are common at some places during the monsoon season (Jul-Sep). Karakoram Highway
  • The Karakoram Highway is best travelled in the spring or early autumn. Karakoram Highway

Permits and fees

  • If you wish to cross borders using the highway, get the visa of the other country beforehand; otherwise entry at the border will not be granted. Karakoram Highway
  • Entry will not be granted to those on cycles. Karakoram Highway
  • The Wakhan Corridor has neither good roads nor border posts where one could legally enter China or Pakistan. Karakoram Highway

Altitude and health

  • Mind altitude sickness; carry an oxygen mask if you have chronic respiratory conditions or when you feel unwell. Karakoram Highway
  • Some parts of the highway are very hot during summer; the temperature can reach 45°C (113°F). Karakoram Highway
  • Carry a first aid kit just in case of an accident. Karakoram Highway

Staying safe

  • The more you go north, the safer it becomes, especially between Gilgit and the border pass. Karakoram Highway
  • Many countries advise against all travel on the highway between Mansehra and Chilas due to poor road safety. Karakoram Highway
  • Beware of avalanches and landslides. Karakoram Highway
