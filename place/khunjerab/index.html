Know before you go

Getting there

  • Buses to and from Tashkurgan stop in Sost, with driving time taking roughly 5 hours. Sost
  • The usual jumping off point for the Karakoram Highway is Gilgit, and you can take a PIA flight from Islamabad to Gilgit. Karakoram Highway
  • The highway runs from Abbottabad to the border in Pakistan, and to Kashgar in China. Karakoram Highway

Getting around

  • You can hire a jeep to enter the Chapursan Valley, where guesthouses exist in some villages. Sost
  • Driving direction changes at the Chinese-Pakistani border. Karakoram Highway
  • There is SCOM cell signal in Sost, but internet is very slow during peak hours. Sost

When to go

  • Check local reports before the trip and avoid adventuring if it rains or snows heavily; extra caution is required in winter. Karakoram Highway
  • Obey suggested speed limits, which change with the season or weather events. Karakoram Highway
  • Winds can be very strong and frigid, so carry a lot of warm clothing. Karakoram Highway

Permits and fees

  • At the Sost immigration office, travelers must go through the entry and exit process and fill out a health declaration form. It can be busy and chaotic, so arrive with plenty of time. Sost

Altitude and health

  • Mind altitude sickness: many people get symptoms already if sleeping at 2,500 m without acclimatization, and 3,500 m is regarded as very high in this context. Carry an oxygen mask if you have chronic respiratory conditions or feel unwell. Karakoram Highway

Money and connectivity

  • Tickets to Tashkurgan from NATCO cost 1500-2000, while Xinjiang Gawadar International costs 2500. Sost

Staying safe

  • The Karakoram Highway lies in one of the safer parts of Pakistan, but expect inhospitable terrain. The more you go north, the safer it becomes, especially between Gilgit and the border pass. Karakoram Highway
  • Many countries advise against all travel on the highway between Mansehra and Chilas via Battagram, Besham City, Dasu and Sazin up to the junction with the N15, due to poor road safety. Karakoram Highway
  • Beware of avalanches and landslides. Before thinking of going on the trip, check local reports and avoid adventuring if it rains or snows heavily. Extra caution is required in the winter. Karakoram Highway
