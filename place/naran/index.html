Know before you go

Getting there

  • Naran is 119 km from Mansehra city and 65 km from Babusar Top pass. Naran
  • Kaghan Valley can be reached by road via Balakot, Mansehra, and Abbottabad, or from Islamabad. Kaghan Valley
  • From Chilas to Naran by bus takes 4 hours and costs Rs 1500 (2025). Naran

Getting around

  • Buses and smaller transport go from town to town, and for side destinations you can find a jeep or walk. Kaghan Valley
  • Towns are small enough to explore on foot. Kaghan Valley
  • Lake Saiful Muluk can be accessed from Naran by jeep on a 14-km road open during summer, about half an hour; if the road is closed or you want to walk, it is an easy, gradual 3-6 hour walk. Kaghan Valley

When to go

  • Kaghan Valley is at its best in the summer months May to September. Kaghan Valley
  • In May the temperature is maximum 11 °C and minimum 3 °C. Kaghan Valley
  • The road beyond Naran is snowbound throughout the winter, and movement is also restricted during the monsoon season. Kaghan Valley

Permits and fees

  • Fishing licenses are issued by the Fisheries Department at Naran. Kaghan Valley
  • There is a Rs 500 (2025) fee to enter the national park at Lake Saiful Muluk. Kaghan Valley

Money and connectivity

  • A bus from Chilas to Naran costs Rs 1500 (2025). Naran
  • A jeep from Naran to Lake Saiful Muluk costs Rs 1500 in 2025. Kaghan Valley

Staying safe

  • At Chilas Zero Point, the Kaghan Valley road closes daily at 17:00 for traffic from the North and opens again early morning. Kaghan Valley
  • Lake Dudipatsar is remote, with almost no people there and no hotels or restaurants. Kaghan Valley
  • The road to Lake Dudipatsar is very difficult. Kaghan Valley
