Know before you go

Getting there

  • Deosai National Park can be accessed from two main routes: Skardu north and Astore west. Deosai National Park
  • A 4WD vehicle is recommended for either route, especially in early summer when snowmelt can affect paths. Deosai National Park
  • From Skardu, the route is uninhabited until Chilam Chowki, where a small summer hotel offers tea and yogurt. Deosai National Park

When to go

  • Between November and May the area of Deosai is snow bound. Sheosar Lake
  • Most visitors come to the lake in the summer, between June and early September. Sheosar Lake
  • The Balti people call it 'Ghbiarsa', meaning 'summer's place', because it is only accessible in the summer. Deosai National Park

Altitude and health

  • The Deosai Plains are situated at an average elevation of 4,114 metres (13,497 ft) above sea level. Deosai National Park
  • Sheosar Lake is located at an elevation of 4,250 metres (13,940 ft). Sheosar Lake

Money and connectivity

  • The fee for visiting Deosai National Park for local tourists is Rs:500 Pkr per person. Deosai National Park

Staying safe

  • Consider a guided tour for added safety and a richer experience. Deosai National Park
