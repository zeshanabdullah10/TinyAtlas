Skardu

Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan

Skardu is a town in the region of the same name in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan. Baltistan's five valleys – Shigar, Skardu, Khaplu, Rondu and Kharmang – host an incomparable cluster of mountain peaks and glaciers, and are noted for their luscious peaches, apricots, apples and pears. Wikipedia
